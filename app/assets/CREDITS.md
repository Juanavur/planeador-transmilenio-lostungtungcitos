# Visual asset credits

Photographs are stored locally here and shown in the single-page hero collage. Optimized WebP renditions are embedded in `app/index.html` because the existing single-file interface can also be served by older running copies of its static HTTP handler; the original local files remain available for direct serving. They are cropped and rendered in monochrome with CSS; those adaptations do not change the underlying transportation data.
